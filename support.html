Support

Keep Bathroom Report free.

No ads. No investors. No data sold. Just one person keeping a map of 84,000+ bathrooms accurate.

Most projects asking for money say "help cover our costs" without saying what those are. Here are mine.

Running the app Servers, database, hosting — a week of real traffic
$0.13
Verifying locations Address lookups, roughly $30 per 1,000 places
$179.73

That's an actual week. Hosting a map is close to free now — three hundred thousand database reads cost twelve cents. What costs money is making the map right: every address checked, every duplicate removed, every closed store pulled.

Nothing here is bulk-imported and left alone. Casey's went from 1,500 records to 2,400 real ones. Kwik Trip went down by 500 when double-counted stores got cleaned out. That work is the product, and it's the only part with a bill attached.

The promise

No ads, ever. Not banners, not sponsored pins, not a chain paying to look cleaner than it is. The moment a business can pay to change what the map says, the map is worthless. That's the whole reason this page exists.
