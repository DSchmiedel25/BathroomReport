BATHROOMREPORT — DATA SOURCES REFERENCE
Compiled 2026-07-29. Everything used or evaluated in one session.
=================================================================

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
Two kinds of source, and they do NOT overlap:

  OpenStreetMap  ->  whether a restroom EXISTS, wheelchair access, changing
                     tables, amenities. Volunteer-surveyed, so a mapper
                     standing there notices the bathroom.

  Brand APIs /    ->  HOURS, addresses, phone, first-party coordinates.
  All the Places      Corporate-published, so hours are accurate and fresh —
                      but they never mention restrooms.

Neither covers the other. That's the whole reason the community layer exists.
Rule adopted today: a chain needs a RESTROOM signal, not just locations,
before it goes on the map.

1. ALL THE PLACES (ATP)  — primary hours source
=================================================================
What:     Open-source project running scrapers against thousands of brands'
          own store locators. Weekly rebuild.
Licence:  CC0-1.0 (public domain, commercial use fine, no attribution needed)
Site:     https://www.alltheplaces.xyz
Spiders:  https://alltheplaces.xyz/spiders.html
Repo:     https://github.com/alltheplaces/alltheplaces
API doc:  https://github.com/alltheplaces/alltheplaces/blob/master/API.md

DOWNLOAD ONE CHAIN (phone-friendly, few MB):
  https://data.alltheplaces.xyz/runs/latest/output/<spider_name>.geojson

  Do NOT download the full build (runs/latest.zip) — 1.4 GB, 4,367 files.

WARNINGS LEARNED THE HARD WAY:
  * Quality varies wildly BY SPIDER. Always dry-run before writing.
  * Some spiders emit TWO records per site: "<ref>_fuel" + "<ref>_store"
    (Casey's) or "<ref>-FUEL" + bare "<ref>" (Stewart's). Hours DIFFER —
    pumps 24h, store closes 22:00. Always keep the STORE record; taking the
    fuel one shows "open" at 2am at a locked building.
  * Whole metros can be missing. Starbucks had ZERO coverage in Manhattan,
    Brooklyn and Boston while fine in Chicago/LA. Verify your area.
  * "@source_uri" on every record reveals the brand's real API. That's how
    the Circle K endpoint was found.

2. CIRCLE K STORE LOCATOR API  — the big outstanding win
=================================================================
What:     Circle K's own public locator. Bounding-box query, no API key.
          Returns "openingHours":{"alwaysOpen":true} etc.
Covers:   Circle K + Holiday + Couche-Tard (one run does all three)
Worth:    6,064 unknown hours = 58% of the entire remaining gap

ENDPOINT:
  https://api.circlek.com/us/ngrp-store-locator/v1/stations
    ?bottomRightLongitude=<E>&bottomRightLatitude=<S>
    &topLeftLongitude=<W>&topLeftLatitude=<N>
    &maxResults=2000&country=US&brand=CIRCLEK,COUCHE_TARD,HOLIDAY

  Caps at maxResults — a full response means the box was truncated, so
  subdivide it. tools/fetch-circlek-hours.js handles that automatically.
  Needs Node 18+ (uses global fetch). Not a phone job.

OFFICIAL ALTERNATIVE (needs a key, sanctioned, documented):
  https://www.developer.circlek.com/api-documentation-main-page
  Use this if the public endpoint starts returning 403/429.

3. NYC OPEN DATA — PUBLIC RESTROOMS  — the model city dataset
=================================================================
What:     City-maintained. Name, coords, type, operator, SEASONALITY, hours,
          ADA level, stall counts, changing stations, access notes.
Dataset:  https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Public-Restrooms/i7jb-7jku
JSON:     https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/i7jb-7jku.json?$limit=5000
Alt:      vzrx-zg6z (operational only), hjae-yuav (parks toilets, older)

RESULT: 1,066 records, 522 matched our 747 ->
  503 hours, 257 accessible, 145 not accessible, 24 limited,
  413 changing stations, 101 seasonal, and 40 NOT OPERATIONAL
  (those were tagged hrs:"closed" — hidden but kept, ids preserved).

CAVEATS:
  * City says availability changes with maintenance/construction and the
    data may not be real-time.
  * Seasonal records give NO dates — 132 flagged, 0 with months. So the app
    shows "⚠️ Seasonal — may be closed in winter" and does NOT compute a
    season. Inventing one would be a guess.
  * Common hours string is "8am-4pm, Open later seasonally" -> written as
    0800-1600. Understates in summer, never overstates. Deliberate.

4. OPENSTREETMAP / OVERPASS  — restroom existence & accessibility
=================================================================
What:     Volunteer-mapped. The ONLY source for things nobody owns:
          highway rest areas, public toilets, park comfort stations.
Tool:     https://overpass-turbo.eu

QUERIES USED:
  Rest areas (US bbox — a lat/lng box is NOT a border, see warning):
    [out:json][timeout:180];
    ( node["highway"="rest_area"](24.5,-125,49.5,-66);
      way["highway"="rest_area"](24.5,-125,49.5,-66);
      relation["highway"="rest_area"](24.5,-125,49.5,-66); );
    out center tags;

  Public toilets:      amenity=toilets
  Toll-road plazas:    highway=services   (NOT rest_area — likely a gap;
                       NY Thruway / PA & OH Turnpike service plazas)

WARNINGS:
  * A BOUNDING BOX IS NOT A BORDER. A "US" box swallows Quebec, Ontario and
    northern Mexico. An export that looked like 158 new US rest areas was
    152 Quebec ("Halte de...") and 6 Mexico. Real answer: ZERO new.
  * OSM rest-area coverage is EXHAUSTED for us — 918 US records, 917 already
    mapped. Remaining lead: 923 previously excluded for "unknown toilets".
  * OSM hours coverage is poor (~19% on rest areas) — that's WHY we were at
    63% unknown. Don't source hours from OSM.

5. STATE DOT DATA  — good attributes, NO hours
=================================================================
No national rest-area dataset exists. It's state by state.

FIND THEM:
  https://hub.arcgis.com/search?q=rest%20area
  Pattern: data-<agency>.opendata.arcgis.com   (e.g. data-wisdot...)
  Fallback: "<state> DOT rest area GIS open data" or data.<state>.gov

PULL WHOLE LAYER AS GEOJSON (skip the download UI):
  https://.../FeatureServer/0/query?where=1=1&outFields=*&f=geojson
  Caps at 1,000 records — page with resultOffset beyond that.

CALTRANS (verified working, last updated 2023-08-15):
  https://caltrans-gis.dot.ca.gov/arcgis/rest/services/CHhighway/Rest_Areas/FeatureServer/0

  Fields: RESTROOM flag, HANDICAP, TRAFFIC_DIRECTION (solves the
  northbound/southbound dedup problem), CITY, ZIPCODE, COUNTY, ROUTE, PHONE.
  NO HOURS FIELD — closure status lives outside the dataset.

  CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER ASSUMPTION: DOT data does NOT bring hours.
  It brings attribute quality. For hours, use brand locators.

8. THE APP'S HOURS FORMAT (get this wrong and it fails silently)
=================================================================
  loc.hrs      "24" | "HHMM-HHMM" | "closed" | "" (unknown)
               PARSED WITH parseInt — "05:00-23:00" reads as 5 and 23,
               i.e. 00:05 to 00:23. FOUR DIGITS, NO COLON.
  loc.hours    per-day map {mon:"0500-2300", ..., sun:"0600-1900"}
               Takes precedence over loc.hrs. Was used by 33 of 22,904
               records before this session.
  loc.seasonal true -> shows the seasonal caveat, does NOT affect status.
  "00:00-24:00" must collapse to "24" or it displays "12:00 AM – 12:00 AM".

  Missing hours are NEVER treated as closed. That's a product rule.
