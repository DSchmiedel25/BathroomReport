COORDINATE CONFLICTS — two real stores sharing one set of coordinates
=====================================================================
Found 2026-07-30 by the cross-chain overlap audit.

These are NOT duplicates. Each pair is two genuinely different street addresses recorded at
IDENTICAL coordinates, which means one pin of each pair is in the wrong place on the map.

Fixing them requires an authoritative coordinate for the misplaced store. That can't be derived
from the data we hold — it needs the chain's store locator or Street View. Left in place rather
than guessed at, because a wrong coordinate that looks confident is worse than one flagged.
