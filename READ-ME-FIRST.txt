WHAT CHANGED
  Seven records carried a bare ZIP code where a street address goes — "14740", "10005",
  " 76063". That is worse than an empty field: it looks like data, tells a driver nothing, and it
  earned five of them a thin /guide/ page.

  The ZIP is now kept where ZIPs belong (meta.zip), the record is flagged meta.addr_status =
  "zip_only" so the backlog is findable, and the address is genuinely empty. One address with a
  leading space was trimmed.

    quickchek      1      racetrac       3  (one of which had the stray space)

  Verified afterwards: 0 zip-only addresses and 0 whitespace issues remain across all 28,087
  records, and all 7 keep their ZIP.
