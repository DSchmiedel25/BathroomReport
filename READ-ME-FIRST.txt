WHY THE UNKNOWN SPLITS THREE WAYS
  Wording them alike would misinform in two directions.

  A metro entry with no access tag has a toilet a human confirmed; what's missing is who may use
  it. Calling that "unconfirmed" would be wrong about a demonstrably real civic toilet.

  A store the source described without listing a restroom — 353 Circle Ks whose own API lists air
  and ATM but omits the restroom it lists for the other 5,950 — almost certainly has one. Lead
  optimistic, let a visitor settle it.

  A bare OSM point, an untagged polygon, or a picnic area is a different bet. "Probably" would be
  a lie: plenty are a pull-off with a bench and a bin. Someone leaving the interstate on that
  promise is the exact failure this app exists to prevent.
